### 1. No Botting
The use of any third-party applications, tools, scripts, or software that automate gameplay or provide an unfair gameplay advantage is strictly prohibited. This includes programs that record and repeat keystrokes or actions.

Gameplay that resembles automated behavior may result in action being taken against your account.

_punishment: perm ban_

### 2. No Abusing Bugs
If you discover a bug that is game breaking and gives an unfair competitive advantage to a player in any playstyle, it must be reported directly to the moderators or Robby instead of posting it in ⁠discord server.

_Punishment: 7 days ➞ 14 days ➞ 30 days ➞ perm ban_

### 3. No Harassment
This is a competitive game. Trash talk and rivalry between players may happen, but targeted harassment is not allowed.
- Normal Harassment: Repeatedly insulting, targeting, or provoking another player beyond normal competitive banter.

_Punishment: 7 days ➞ 14 days ➞ 30 days ➞ perm ban_

- Sexual Harassment: Unwanted sexual comments, advances, or sexually explicit remarks directed at another player after they have asked you to stop, or behavior intended to make others uncomfortable.

_Punishment: 30 days ➞ perm ban_

<small> Note: Context and intent will be considered when determining harassment.</small>

### 4. Intentional Crashing the Game
- Not sure why players think its funny to crash the game.

_Punishment: 14 days ➞ 30 days ➞ perm ban_

Note: This does not apply to GvG Guild Tiles

### 5. No Doxxing
No leaking or sharing another player's private or personally identifiable information without their permission.
- Posting another player's full legal/government name in dms, guild chat, global chat, and bullhorns is prohibited.

_Punishment: 30 days ➞ perm ban_

- Sharing highly sensitive personal information intended to expose another player's identity (e.g. full legal name + address or phone number), is considered full doxxing.

_Punishment: perm ban_

### 6. Use Common Sense
- No spamming racist words via the bull-horn or in global chat.
- No hacking, duping, ddosing

_Punishment: perm ban_

### 7. GvG Guild Tiles
- Using more than one account on a single device to interact with guild tiles is not permitted. Each player must use only one account per device to ensure fair participation and prevent abuse.

> Players using multiple accounts across different devices may be asked to demonstrate that their gameplay is not automated if suspicious activity is reported or observed by the moderation team. Failure to reasonably verify manual gameplay may result in mod action.

_Punishment: perm ban_

## How to Appeal
To appeal a punishment, contact the moderators via discord DMs.

Appeals are only intended for situations where you believe a moderator made a mistake in applying a punishment. Appeals are **not** for requesting another chance or asking for a reduced penalty.

## Understanding the Punishment System
Punishments escalate across all rule violations and do not reset between different types of offenses. Each new violation moves the account to the next punishment tier.

Examples:
- If a player commits bug abuse and receives a **7-day ban**, then later commits normal harassment, the next punishment escalates to a **14-day ban**.

- If a player commits **sexual harassment (30-day ban)** and later commits another violation such as bug abuse or normal harassment, the next punishment will be a **permanent ban**.

Repeated violations will continue to escalate and may result in a permanent ban.
